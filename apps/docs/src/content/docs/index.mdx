## Why Choose Ssarg Launcher?

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Unified Modrinth + CurseForge Search" icon="magnifier">
    Search and browse modpacks, mods, shaders, and resource packs across both Modrinth and CurseForge simultaneously in one clean interface.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Smart Download Ranking" icon="star">
    Results are automatically sorted by total download count descending (`20M+ → 3M+ → 100K+...`), ensuring legendary packs like *All the Mods*, *FTB*, and *RLCraft* are always at the top.
  </Card>
  <Card title="100% Ad-Free & Zero Telemetry" icon="approve-check-circle">
